Amitabh Chaudhry [Management]:
Executive Strategic Summary & Operating Performance:
Good evening and thank you for joining. In Q1 FY25, Axis Bank delivered a Net Profit (PAT) of ₹6555 crore, reflecting a YoY growth of 17%. Our Return on Assets (ROA) expanded to 1.86% and consolidated Return on Equity (ROE) stood at 19.85%.

Strategic Execution under GPS Pillars:
1. Resilient Franchise: Total balance sheet size crossed ₹15.5 lakh crore. Branch network expanded to 5040 branches.
2. Distinctiveness & Digital Adoption: Monthly active users on our flagship retail app 'open' reached 16.3 million. Our wholesale digital platform 'NEO' now handles over 68% of corporate cash management volumes.
3. Customer Experience: In the independent Cantor benchmarking study, Axis Bank secured the #2 rank in Net Promoter Score (NPS) across peer private banks.

Puneet Sharma [Management]:
Financial Analytics & Asset Quality Metrics:
1. Revenue & Margins: Net Interest Income (NII) for the quarter grew 15% YoY to ₹13430 crore. Net Interest Margin (NIM) was 3.97%. Core Operating Profit rose to ₹10580 crore. Cost-to-Income ratio improved to 47.6%.

2. Balance Sheet Yields & Costs: Yield on advances stood at 9.74%, while cost of funds was 5.37%, resulting in a healthy interest spread of 4.37%.

3. Asset Quality & Capital Ratios: Gross NPA ratio declined by 4 bps QoQ to 1.46%, and Net NPA ratio stood at 0.39%. Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR) on gross NPAs reached 77.7%. Annualized credit cost was 57 bps.

4. Capital & Liquidity: Basel III Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR) stood at 16.85%, with CET-1 ratio at 14.41%. Loan-to-Deposit Ratio (LDR) was controlled at 88.0%.

Mahrukh Adajania (HSBC) [Analyst]:
Analytical Query - Deposit Repricing, LDR & Citi Integration Synergy:
Thank you. I have two specific analytical questions:
Q1: Given system liquidity conditions, what is your guidance on terminal cost of deposits, and how fast will LDR reach your targeted 85% mark?
Q2: For Rajiv—could you quantify the wealth management AUM growth, cross-sell ratio, and annual churn rate within the acquired Citi customer base?

Rajiv Anand [Management]:
Response on Citi Integration Metrics & Wealth Management AUM:
Thanks Mahrukh. On Citi integration:
- Wealth Management AUM: Overall AUM under 'Burgundy' and Citi wealth accounts increased to ₹4.64 lakh crore, representing a YoY growth of 25%.
- Attrition Rate: Customer retention post-integration remains best-in-class, with annual attrition under 1.8% across credit cards and high-net-worth accounts.
- Cross-Sell Multiplier: Cross-sell ratio of liability products and home loans to Citi cardholders increased by 35% YoY, delivering higher fee intensity per customer.

Puneet Sharma [Management]:
Response on LDR Trajectory & Deposit Cost Guidance:
Regarding LDR and cost of funds:
- CASA & Deposit Breakdown: CASA ratio stood at 41.8%. Retail term deposits grew 20% YoY, providing granular liquidity support.
- LDR Target: LDR reduced to 88.0% this quarter. We are growing advances at 13% while targeting deposit growth at 15%, ensuring LDR smoothly converges to 85% over the next 2-3 quarters.
- Cost of Funds Trajectory: Incremental cost of deposits has plateaued, with sequential increase contained to just 3 bps.

Suresh Ganapathy (Macquarie) [Analyst]:
Analytical Query - Unsecured Credit Stress, Delinquencies & RBI Risk Weights:
My question is on unsecured retail assets (Personal Loans & Credit Cards). What is the current share of unsecured loans in overall advances, what are the 30+ and 90+ DPD delinquency trends, and how did the RBI risk weight recalibration impact CET-1?

Sumit Bali [Management]:
Response on Unsecured Retail Portfolio & Risk Analytics:
Hi Suresh, Sumit here. Here are the precise retail lending metrics:
- Portfolio Mix: Unsecured retail advances (personal loans + credit cards) represent exactly 10.4% of total bank advances.
- Customer Profile: Over 83% of personal loan borrowers are salaried individuals working with ET 500 / MNC corporates, or existing high-vintage deposit customers.
- Delinquency Trends: Gross 30+ DPD stands at 1.48% and 90+ DPD delinquency is flat at 0.55%, demonstrating high credit stability.
- Capital Impact: The RBI risk weight increase had a one-time impact of ~71 bps on CET-1, which we have fully absorbed through organic internal capital generation of 45 bps per fiscal year.

Munish Sharda [Management]:
