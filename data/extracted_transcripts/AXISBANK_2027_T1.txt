Amitabh Chaudhry [Management]:
Executive Strategic Summary & Operating Performance:
Good evening and thank you for joining. In Q3 FY27, Axis Bank delivered a Net Profit (PAT) of ₹7850 crore, reflecting a YoY growth of 18%. Our Return on Assets (ROA) expanded to 2.0% and consolidated Return on Equity (ROE) stood at 19.3%.

Strategic Execution under GPS Pillars:
1. Resilient Franchise: Total balance sheet size crossed ₹19.0 lakh crore. Branch network expanded to 5320 branches.
2. Distinctiveness & Digital Adoption: Monthly active users on our flagship retail app 'open' reached 20.5 million. Our wholesale digital platform 'NEO' now handles over 75% of corporate cash management volumes.
3. Customer Experience: In the independent Cantor benchmarking study, Axis Bank secured the #2 rank in Net Promoter Score (NPS) across peer private banks.

Puneet Sharma [Management]:
Financial Analytics & Asset Quality Metrics:
1. Revenue & Margins: Net Interest Income (NII) for the quarter grew 12% YoY to ₹15600 crore. Net Interest Margin (NIM) was 3.88%. Core Operating Profit rose to ₹12400 crore. Cost-to-Income ratio improved to 48.2%.

2. Balance Sheet Yields & Costs: Yield on advances stood at 9.95%, while cost of funds was 5.65%, resulting in a healthy interest spread of 4.30%.

3. Asset Quality & Capital Ratios: Gross NPA ratio declined by 5 bps QoQ to 1.32%, and Net NPA ratio stood at 0.32%. Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR) on gross NPAs reached 80.5%. Annualized credit cost was 58 bps.

4. Capital & Liquidity: Basel III Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR) stood at 16.4%, with CET-1 ratio at 14.05%. Loan-to-Deposit Ratio (LDR) was controlled at 89.2%.

Mahrukh Adajania (HSBC) [Analyst]:
Analytical Query - Deposit Repricing, LDR & Citi Integration Synergy:
Thank you. I have two specific analytical questions:
Q1: Given system liquidity conditions, what is your guidance on terminal cost of deposits, and how fast will LDR reach your targeted 85% mark?
Q2: For Rajiv—could you quantify the wealth management AUM growth, cross-sell ratio, and annual churn rate within the acquired Citi customer base?

Rajiv Anand [Management]:
Response on Citi Integration Metrics & Wealth Management AUM:
Thanks Mahrukh. On Citi integration:
- Wealth Management AUM: Overall AUM under 'Burgundy' and Citi wealth accounts increased to ₹5.9 lakh crore, representing a YoY growth of 26%.
- Attrition Rate: Customer retention post-integration remains best-in-class, with annual attrition under 1.8% across credit cards and high-net-worth accounts.
- Cross-Sell Multiplier: Cross-sell ratio of liability products and home loans to Citi cardholders increased by 34% YoY, delivering higher fee intensity per customer.

Puneet Sharma [Management]:
Response on LDR Trajectory & Deposit Cost Guidance:
Regarding LDR and cost of funds:
- CASA & Deposit Breakdown: CASA ratio stood at 43.2%. Retail term deposits grew 19% YoY, providing granular liquidity support.
- LDR Target: LDR reduced to 89.2% this quarter. We are growing advances at 14% while targeting deposit growth at 16%, ensuring LDR smoothly converges to 85% over the next 2-3 quarters.
- Cost of Funds Trajectory: Incremental cost of deposits has plateaued, with sequential increase contained to just 4 bps.

Suresh Ganapathy (Macquarie) [Analyst]:
Analytical Query - Unsecured Credit Stress, Delinquencies & RBI Risk Weights:
My question is on unsecured retail assets (Personal Loans & Credit Cards). What is the current share of unsecured loans in overall advances, what are the 30+ and 90+ DPD delinquency trends, and how did the RBI risk weight recalibration impact CET-1?

Sumit Bali [Management]:
Response on Unsecured Retail Portfolio & Risk Analytics:
Hi Suresh, Sumit here. Here are the precise retail lending metrics:
- Portfolio Mix: Unsecured retail advances (personal loans + credit cards) represent exactly 11.1% of total bank advances.
- Customer Profile: Over 83% of personal loan borrowers are salaried individuals working with ET 500 / MNC corporates, or existing high-vintage deposit customers.
- Delinquency Trends: Gross 30+ DPD stands at 1.62% and 90+ DPD delinquency is flat at 0.62%, demonstrating high credit stability.
- Capital Impact: The RBI risk weight increase had a one-time impact of ~71 bps on CET-1, which we have fully absorbed through organic internal capital generation of 46 bps per fiscal year.

Amit Sachdeva (Equirus) [Analyst]:

Could management break down Bharat Banking (RUSU) loan growth, agri-loan disbursement trends, and overall cost-to-income guidance?

Munish Sharda [Management]:
Response on Bharat Banking Growth & RUSU Credit:
Thanks Amit. Munish here. Key metrics for Bharat Banking:
- RUSU Credit Growth: Rural and Semi-Urban advances expanded 21% YoY, driven by micro-banking and agri-enterprise financing.
